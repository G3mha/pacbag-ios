# Privacy Policy for PacBag

Last updated: [Current Date]

## Overview
PacBag ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our iOS application.

## Information We Collect
PacBag does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers. All your data remains private and under your control.

## Data Storage
- **iCloud Storage**: All your packing lists, trips, and items are stored in your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit service
- **Local Storage**: App preferences and settings are stored locally on your device
- **Photos**: Any photos you add to items are stored in your iCloud account

## Data Usage
- Your data is only accessible by you through your iCloud account
- We cannot see, access, or retrieve your personal packing lists or trip information
- Data syncs between your devices using your iCloud account

## Push Notifications
- PacBag uses local notifications to remind you about upcoming trips
- These notifications are scheduled on your device and do not involve any external servers
- You can disable notifications at any time in Settings

## Third-Party Services
PacBag uses the following Apple services:
- **CloudKit**: For syncing data between your devices
- **Photos**: For adding images to items (with your permission)

## Data Security
Your data is protected by:
- Apple's iCloud security measures
- Your device passcode/biometric authentication
- End-to-end encryption provided by iCloud

## Children's Privacy
PacBag does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date.

## Your Rights
