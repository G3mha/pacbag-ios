Bags now have a weight of their own.

A packed suitcase weighs what is in it plus the suitcase. PacBag only counted the contents, so a bag reading 19 of 20 kg could still be over at the check-in desk. New bags start from a sensible empty weight for their type, you can set it yourself on any bag, and the bag screen now breaks the total into bag, items and total.

The add and edit screens are easier to read, and the app no longer closes itself when a save fails.
