Privacy Policy for PacBag

Last updated: December 2024

Your privacy is important to us. PacBag is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal data. All information remains private in your iCloud account.

1. Information Collection and Use

PacBag does NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. The app operates with a privacy-first approach:

  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No data mining
  • No third-party data sharing

2. Data Storage and Sync

iCloud Storage (CloudKit)

PacBag uses Apple's CloudKit service to store and sync your data:

  • All trips, bags, items, and packing lists are stored in your personal iCloud container
  • Data syncs automatically between your devices signed into the same iCloud account
  • Only you can access your data through your Apple ID
  • We cannot see, access, or retrieve any of your information
  • Apple's privacy policy governs iCloud data: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Local Storage

Some information is stored locally on your device:

  • App preferences and settings
  • Temporary cache data
  • Notification schedules

3. Photos and Media

When you add photos to items:

  • Photos are stored in your iCloud Photo Library
  • The app only accesses photos you explicitly select
  • Photo access requires your explicit permission
  • You can revoke photo access anytime in iOS Settings

4. Notifications

PacBag uses local notifications:

  • Reminders are scheduled locally on your device
  • No push notification servers are used
  • No notification data is transmitted externally
  • You control all notification settings

5. Data Security

Your data is protected by:

  • Apple's iCloud security infrastructure
  • End-to-end encryption for iCloud data
  • Your device passcode/Face ID/Touch ID
  • iOS app sandboxing

6. Data Control and Deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • Delete individual items, bags, or trips at any time
  • Use "Reset App" in settings to delete all data
  • Disable iCloud sync in your device's iCloud settings
  • Deleting the app removes all local data
  • Turn off iCloud for PacBag to stop syncing

7. Children's Privacy

PacBag is rated 4+ and suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. The app does not collect any personal information from any users.

8. International Users

Your data is stored in Apple's iCloud servers in the region associated with your Apple ID. Data location and protection are governed by Apple's policies and local regulations.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. No Third-Party Services

PacBag does not use any third-party services except for Apple's iOS frameworks:

  • CloudKit for data sync
  • PhotosUI for image selection
  • UserNotifications for local reminders

11. Your Consent

By using PacBag, you consent to this privacy policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

12. California Privacy Rights

California residents have specific rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Since PacBag does not collect personal information, these rights are inherently protected.

13. European Union Users (GDPR)

For EU users, PacBag complies with GDPR by design:

  • No personal data collection
  • Data minimization (only essential data for app function)
  • User control over all data
  • Right to deletion (delete anytime)
  • Data portability (via iCloud)
