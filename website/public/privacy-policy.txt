PRIVACY POLICY FOR PACBAG

Last Updated: December 2024

OVERVIEW
--------
PacBag respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or have access to any of your personal data. All information remains private in your iCloud account.

WHAT INFORMATION WE COLLECT
---------------------------
We collect NO personal information. PacBag:
- Does not require user accounts
- Does not track users
- Does not show advertisements
- Does not share data with third parties
- Does not use analytics

HOW YOUR DATA IS STORED
-----------------------
iCloud (CloudKit):
- Your trips, bags, and packing lists are stored in your personal iCloud account
- Only you can access this data with your Apple ID
- We cannot see or access your information
- Data syncs between your devices automatically

Local Storage:
- App settings stored on device
- Notification schedules stored locally

PHOTOS
------
- Photos you add are stored in your iCloud Photo Library
- App only accesses photos you select
- Requires your permission
- Can be disabled in iOS Settings

NOTIFICATIONS
-------------
- All reminders are local notifications
- No external push notification servers
- You control all notification settings

DATA SECURITY
-------------
Protected by:
- Apple's iCloud security
- Your device passcode/Face ID/Touch ID
- iOS app sandboxing
- End-to-end encryption

YOUR RIGHTS
-----------
You can:
- Delete any data at any time
- Reset entire app in Settings
- Disable iCloud sync
- Delete app to remove all local data

CHILDREN
--------
PacBag is suitable for all ages (4+) and does not collect information from anyone.

CONTACT
-------
For privacy questions: privacy@pacbagapp.com

SUMMARY
-------
Your data stays in your iCloud account. We cannot access it. You have complete control.
